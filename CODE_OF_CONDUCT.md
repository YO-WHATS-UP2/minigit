# Code of Conduct

minigit follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and remember that everyone here is learning, including the maintainers. Review the code, not the person. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

To report a problem, message a project mentor privately on the Hello FOSS Discord, or contact the Hello FOSS organising team.
